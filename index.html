Xinjing Zhou(周信静)

I am a Postdoc in MIT’s Data Systems Group, working with Samuel Madden on agentic data systems. I completed my PhD in the same group, advised by Mike Stonebraker. My research focuses on building data systems for AI agents: long-running, stateful, and often speculative applications that interact with databases, file systems, vector stores, and external tools. More broadly, my work spans database systems, operating systems, and systems for AI, drawing on my PhD work on practical DB–OS co-design, kernel bypass, memory management, networking, and high-performance database engines.
